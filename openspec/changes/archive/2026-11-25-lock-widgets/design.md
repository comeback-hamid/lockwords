## Context
Limits: `docs/widget-limits.md`. The widget code (`expo-widgets`) has no network, no timers and no imports; it only draws the props of the current timeline entry. So the server makes the plan, the app carries it, the widget shows it.

## Decisions
- **A look is a 30-minute slot. Why 30:** a day has 16 waking hours = 32 slots. With 5 looks per word that is about 6 hard words a day, which fits a learner who swipes a few words Hard per round. With 60 minutes only 3 words a day would get their looks, and a hard word would wait days for its turn. Shorter than 30 minutes and a word would often change before the person looked at the phone even once. 30 is the low end of Hamid's range (30 to 60); it is one constant on the server (`SLOT_MINUTES`), easy to change.
- **Words take turns in a group of 6** instead of 5 slots in a row. One word then comes back about every 3 hours, which is better for memory than 2.5 hours in one block, and the lock screen is not the same for a whole morning. With fewer than 6 words the group is smaller; with one word it repeats.
- **Night slots do not count.** From 23:00 to 07:00 the widget keeps the last word of the evening and no look is counted, because nobody has the chance to see it.
- **Only hard words** (Hamid, 2026-11-25). Order (the server decides, the app never sorts): fewest looks first, then the oldest card. A word is hard as the Review rules say (swiped Hard, until Stage 3). Known and removed words are never in.
- **The group must survive a new plan.** The app asks for a new plan every time it opens, so the group is not kept anywhere: it is worked out again from the looks. Words whose looks have started are in; the oldest waiting cards fill it up to 6 (`pickGroup`). Found in review: with a plain "fewest looks first" sort, every reopening pushed the waiting words in and 12 words took turns instead of 6.
- **After 5 looks** a word leaves the group and the next word in the order comes in. When every hard word had its 5 looks, a new lap starts with the same order, so the widget is never empty while hard words exist. A word swiped Hard again starts at 0 looks.
- **Looks never touch SRS.** No stage, no due date and no hard mark changes because of the widget. They live in their own table.
- **The server counts by time.** It keeps the plan it handed out (`widget_plans`: user, slots, made at). On the next call it adds one look for every kept slot whose end has passed (`widget_looks`: user, card, looks, lap), then makes a new plan from now. It cannot know whether the widget is on the lock screen; a learner without the widget just rotates unseen.
- **48 hours per plan** (about 64 slots, plus one last entry "Open Lockwords · for your hard words"). The app refreshes the plan whenever it is opened, which is free of the iOS refresh budget.
- **The pose is not a rule.** The server sends `look` (1 to 5). The widget maps it to Kai's pose: 1 greeting, 2 idle, 3 thinking, 4 speaking, 5 celebrate. Kai has no animation in a widget; the new pose with each slot is what makes it feel alive.
- **One widget, two lock families.** One `expo-widgets` widget "Lockwords" lists `accessoryRectangular` and `accessoryInline`. No circular: it cannot hold a word, and Hamid wants no card count (the home sizes join in `home-widgets`). The layout reads `environment.widgetFamily`.
- **No Kai next to long text** (Hamid, 2026-11-25): the app leaves Kai's picture out of an entry whose word is over 12 letters or whose meaning is over 14 (`roomForKai` in `src/widgets/timeline.ts`); the layout then gives the text the whole width. Still longer text gets a smaller font, then "…".
- **Kai on the lock screen is a one-color picture.** iOS keeps only brightness there, so Kai's body is white and the eyes are darker. The five poses are PNG files made from `assets/kai/*.svg` by a script, shipped with the app and copied once into the shared widget folder. File pictures work in the widget (seen on the simulator, 2026-11-25).
- **Font: the iPhone's own font** on the lock screen (decided while building, 2026-11-25). Mona Sans there needs a plugin of ours that changes the widget target's Xcode project; not built. See `tasks.md` 3.2.
- **Tap:** a word entry carries `widgetURL("lockwords://review?first=<cardId>")`. The app opens the Review tab and starts the round at once (no start screen) with `GET /v1/review?first=<cardId>`. The server puts that card first. The "no hard words" and "open the app" entries open the Review tab as it is.
- **A tapped word that is not due today.** A hard word was swiped Hard, so it is due tomorrow, and today the rules refuse a swipe on it. Decided by Hamid on 2026-11-25 ("look only"): the card still shows first as a **look card**: flip, speaker and Use it work, the three swipes are replaced by one "Next" button, and its level does not change. If the word is due today it is a normal first card.
- **The time zone** comes with the call, like every review call. The day window is 07:00 to 23:00 in that zone.

## Risks
- **Real iPhone signing** (free personal team): may block the app group. Then the widget is simulator-only until the paid account exists; written in `docs/tech-debt.md` as blocked on Hamid.
- **The plan goes stale** when the app is not opened: after 48 hours the widget shows the "open the app" entry. A background refresh is a later change.
- **Words on a locked phone** are visible to anyone who sees the screen. They are only vocabulary; accepted.
